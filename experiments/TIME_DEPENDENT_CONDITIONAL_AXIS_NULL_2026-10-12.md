# Time-dependent conditional axis: an exact pathwise no-record criterion

**2026-10-12 — P1 / EXP-2026-001.** Ordinary-unitary red-team derivation. Reference baseline: approved PSF manuscript 9.0.2, September 7, 2026; in particular Sections 8, 10 and 11. No selector physics is established here.

## Exact criterion

Let a system pointer's two branches drive one closed finite-dimensional environment through H_±(t) = H0(t) ± B(t)/2, with a shared initial pure state |ψ0>. Write P0 = |ψ0><ψ0|; let U0(t) be the propagator generated by H0(t); and put B_I(t) = U0(t)† B(t) U0(t). Let V(t) = |<ψ_-(t)|ψ_+(t)>|.

**Claim:** V(t)=1 for **every time** in an interval exactly when [B_I(t),P0]=0 for every time in that interval (under ordinary regularity of the Hamiltonians). For a qubit, a fixed pure Bloch vector n is blind throughout only if the nonzero interaction-picture conditional axes b_I(t) are collinear with n. An eigenstate of an endpoint effective gate, or of B(0) alone, does not suffice.

**Proof:** If V(t)=1 continuously, the branch rank-one projectors are identical: P+(t)=P-(t)=P(t). Subtracting their von Neumann equations gives [B(t),P(t)]=0. Their common evolution then satisfies P'(t)=-i[H0(t),P(t)], with solution P(t)=U0(t)P0U0(t)†. This proves necessity. Conversely, if [B_I(t),P0]=0, this transported P(t) solves both branch projector equations, yielding identical rays and hence V(t)=1. This is elementary finite-dimensional unitary dynamics, not a novel fundamental theorem.

## Exact counterexample: four analog conditional-axis segments

For H0=0, initial |+X>, and four segments with signed integrated conditional generators φX, φY, -φY, -φX, the branch-overlap magnitudes at input and each boundary are

    (1, 1, |cos φ|, 1, 1).

Both branch unitaries equal the identity at the endpoint; at φ=π/2 the environmental branches are orthogonal after segment two. Wolfram exact Pauli-matrix multiplication returned complex overlaps (1, exp(-iφ), exp(-iφ)cosφ, exp(-iφ), 1), and independent scipy matrix evolution reproduced the magnitudes. Thus even an analog, piecewise-native control path may write and erase perfect intermediate records while remaining endpoint-null.

## Smooth rotating axis

For H0=0 and B(t)=Ω[cos(ωt)X+sin(ωt)Y], initially |+X>, exact rotating-frame SU(2) evolution gives

    V(t)^2 = 1 - [Ω²ω²/(Ω²+ω²)²] [1-cos(t sqrt(Ω²+ω²))]².

Therefore Σ(t)=-log V(t) = Ω²ω²t⁴/8 - Ω²ω²(Ω²+ω²)t⁶/48 + O(t⁸). This was independently checked with Wolfram symbolic series and numerical time ordering. For Ω=1.7, ω=0.9, numeric Σ/t⁴ at t=0.4,0.2,0.1,0.05 was approximately 0.2668,0.2856,0.2908,0.2922, converging toward the analytic coefficient 0.2926125.

For differentiable H0,B with B(0)|ψ0>=b|ψ0>, put K=dot B(0)+i[H0(0),B(0)] (the interaction-picture slope). Then Σ(t)=t⁴ Var_{ψ0}(K)/8+O(t⁵). The earlier static common-drift commutator term is the dot B=0 special case. Random finite-dimensional 3×3 simulations gave the predicted leading coefficient as t→0.

An ideal constructive repair is to add H0(t)=ωZ/2 for the rotating-axis example. Then B_I(t)=ΩX is constant, so |+X> remains pathwise blind. This does *not* certify the control hardware, since the extra drive, spectators, leaked levels and control modes can themselves carry records.

## Implications and limits

The native-gate experiment must characterize its time-dependent common and conditional Hamiltonians in the actual scheduled context, transform into the common-interaction frame, and either produce a shared eigenvector throughout or prospectively bound its nonzero record action. Time-dependent Hamiltonian reconstruction on two superconducting transmons has been experimentally demonstrated (Siva et al., PRX Quantum 4, 040324, 2023; DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.040324), but continuous weak measurements add backaction and cannot simply be presumed mechanism-null in the target protocol. Characterization must account for calibration/target transfer and unseen degrees of freedom.

This analysis covers only closed pure conditional-environment branches. It does not enlarge the Section 8 finite-dimensional shared-local-CPTP-slot certificate into native-device certification. Nor does it decide whether Section 11's optional selector responds to transient physical records or only a designated cumulative-access stage. That remains an independent physical postulate/dynamics question. No extra empirical support for the optional selector follows.
