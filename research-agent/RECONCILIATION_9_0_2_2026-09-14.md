## Reconciled conclusions

1. **No inherited red-team result is overturned by 9.0.2.** The main changes are attribution and scope, not reversal of the prior negative findings.
2. **C4 should be treated as historical research rather than a current-manuscript AQFT claim.** The current paper contains modular/Takesaki structure but not the earlier half-sided-modular-inclusion treatment.
3. **C5 survives only in a narrower current form.** Exact equivariance is for state-preserving record-algebra symmetries; arbitrary delayed-choice operations are expressly outside the theorem. The constructive future-fixed algebra in Sec. 11 is a different, declared-admissibility construction.
4. **Sections 7, 8 and 10 are genuine additions to the current baseline.** They respectively add a quantitative holonomy certificate, a complete compatible target set inside a declared local-noise model, and a specified ordinary registration/memory interaction.
5. **The strongest experimental blocker remains identifiability against the complete ordinary model actually justified by device calibration.** Section 8 solves that problem only for its declared shared environment-local slot; Section 10 supplies a useful ordinary model but not a proof that a device obeys that model.
6. **The compiled-path result and P7 now meet at a sharper boundary.** The manuscript defines a stage-wise stored evidence action for the phenomenological selector echo, while the red-team result asks whether an extra degree of freedom would respond to transient physical records created and erased during implementation. A complete selector dynamics, or an explicitly justified endpoint/designated-stage rule, is still missing.
