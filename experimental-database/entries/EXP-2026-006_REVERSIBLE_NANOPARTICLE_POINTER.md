# EXP-2026-006 — Reversible nanoparticle pointer: proposed collapse/echo platform

- **Proposed status:** active concept, L1 (formalized comparative experiment, not a demonstrated PSF test).
- **Platform:** coherently driven microscopic two-branch system coupled to a trapped mesoscopic nanoparticle pointer.
- **Public precedent:** Peter Renkel, arXiv:2606.22707 (v1 submitted June 2026, later revised August 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22707 . A proposed device and sensitivity calculation, **not** a demonstrated experiment. Title and parameters differ across preprint versions.
- **Ordinary prediction:** a controlled reversible measurement-like pointer displacement and rejoining can restore coherence if the full relevant unitary evolution is reversed and ordinary environmental errors are controlled.
- **Existing rival proposal:** Continuous Spontaneous Localization (CSL) predicts persistent loss proportional to its own model-specific exposure law. This is not the PSF selector equation.
- **Optional PSF hypothesis:** requires separate prior specification of record overlap V_pre, accumulated action Σ, stage/trajectory rule, independently fixed η, and what physical inverse leaves selector degrees uncomputed. Do not infer selector predictions from CSL formulas.
- **Readout:** return fringe/coherence visibility of the microscopic source after a pointer interaction cycle.
- **Leading ordinary confounders:** motional heating, gas/scattering, force and trap mismatch, untracked environmental which-path information, thermal pointer mixture, drift and imperfect reversal. Experimental calibration and held-out prediction regions are absent.
- **Principal next step:** map the proposed pointer trajectory to a declared PSF record algebra and calculate Σ under alternative endpoint-vs-trajectory definitions, while preserving every ordinary noise process compatible with independent pointer controls. A genuine protocol must distinguish both CSL-specific and PSF-specific predictions from ordinary errors without conflating the two proposed physical laws.
- **Readiness rationale:** L1; the related published *proposal* has an explicit pointer-and-reversal idea, but PSF-specific identifiability and implementation are not established.
