## 1. Research thesis

PSF models an observer's accessible past using increasing algebras of records. The current manuscript separates the base record-filtration framework and its mathematical consequences from stronger physical choices concerning future stability, observer-relative actuality, and an optional selector law. The base framework does not modify unitary quantum mechanics and does not by itself predict failure of a complete microscopic echo.

## 4. Experimental discriminator state — P1

The optional selector law remains a **conditional** new-physics proposal, not base-PSF content. With the manuscript definition `Sigma=-log V_pre` and a predeclared common `eta>0`, the conditional selector echo gives `nu_echo=exp(-eta Sigma)=V_pre^eta` after an ideal inverse restores all ordinary non-selector branch degrees while leaving the extra selector degree untouched. Standard unitary QM and base PSF give a complete ideal echo under a genuinely global exact inverse.

The strongest inherited ordinary nulls remain active:

- In a product-record model with `V_pre=|cos(theta)|^N`, a coherent inverse-amplitude mismatch `epsilon` gives `nu_noise=|cos(epsilon theta)|^N`, with apparent `eta_eff=log|cos(epsilon theta)|/log|cos(theta)|`; `N` cancels from this apparent exponent.
- For the locally matched adversarial null `epsilon=sqrt(eta)`, selector and null agree through quadratic order. The log difference begins at `N eta(1-eta)theta^4/12+O(theta^6)`, and a binary X/parity readout has per-shot KL divergence `N eta(1-eta)^2 theta^6/288+O(theta^8)`. The weak-record regime is therefore information-poor against this null.
- The 2026-08-31 common-eigenstate Ramsey construction remains a promising high-information calibration for coherent mismatch at the effective-gate level.
- The 2026-09-07 compiled-path audit remains valid: an exact digital realization can have final branch overlap one while transiently creating orthogonal branch records. A mechanism-null control must therefore be audited over the mechanism-relevant physical trajectory unless the selector dynamics explicitly and defensibly makes only a designated stage relevant.
- Section 8 of 9.0.2 materially improves ordinary compatible-set inference for one declared local slot but does not certify a real native device against broader joint/contextual/memory processes.
- Section 10 supplies an ordinary registration/memory interaction and calibration structure; it does not generate a selector-specific residual and fully recoheres under complete ordinary reversal.

**Current P1 requirement:** declare the native controls, preparation/readout, timing/context and physical filtration; characterize the relevant trajectory including leaked levels, spectators/control modes and retained memory; preserve every ordinary process compatible with calibration; obtain a target-prediction identifiability certificate with uncertainty; independently fix or prospectively calibrate `eta`; only then optimize held-out target conditions and shot counts.

## 8. Epistemic rules

- **Proved manuscript claim**, **independently verified/corroborated result**, **standard theorem**, **derived consequence**, **conjecture**, **interpretation**, and **optional physical postulate** are distinct statuses.
- Literature similarity is not prior art until assumptions, chronology and conclusions are compared.
- Failure to locate prior art is not proof of novelty.
- Source identity and actual source access must be verified before attribution.
- Failed attacks and negative results are preserved.
- A favorable residual is not evidence for new physics unless the strongest relevant ordinary null is prospectively constrained.
- Coarse circuit-depth/gate-count matching is insufficient when nuisance exposure depends on the interaction itself.
- Endpoint nulling is insufficient for a path-sensitive mechanism; audit the mechanism-relevant trajectory or explicitly justify endpoint/designated-stage dependence.
- Statistical power and nuisance identifiability must be designed together. Do not optimize shot counts before the target prediction is identified under the complete justified calibration-compatible family.
- Validation-companion agreement improves evidence only to the extent that the validation reconstructs the claim rather than copying canonical conclusions; repository separation alone is not epistemic independence.
