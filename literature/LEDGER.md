## Assessed literature

### L-2026-001 — Girard, Cheng & Cao, “Demystifying Objectivity with Operator Algebra Quantum Error Correction” (2026)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct/adjacent.
- **PSF claims touched:** N1, N2, P2, P5.
- **What overlaps:** Uses recoverable logical von Neumann subalgebras on environment fragments to characterize what information is locally accessible; identifies nontrivial centers as classically retrievable information and defines redundancy by common central logical observables recoverable on disjoint fragments. This is substantive operator-algebraic treatment of accessible classical records/objectivity.
- **What differs:** The paper is organized around OAQEC/local recoverability and fragment partitions, not an increasing temporal filtration of accessible past-record algebras with compatible record realizations, state-preserving expectation towers, or PSF's root-fidelity visibility envelope. No theorem-level duplication of the PSF temporal filtration was found in this pass.
- **Novelty threat:** medium to the broad wording “operator-algebraic accessible records/objectivity”; lower to the narrower temporal-filtration-plus-visibility synthesis. v1 (2026-06-04) and v2 (2026-06-24) postdate the November 2025 PSF preprint but precede the July 2026 revision, so priority must be assessed claim-by-claim rather than inferred from the July manuscript date.
- **Useful contribution:** Provides a precise neighboring algebraic language (recoverable subalgebras, centers, redundancy) and scalable code models for comparison and possible PSF examples.
- **Reopen if:** full theorem-by-theorem priority comparison or underlying pre-2025 OAQEC antecedents show equivalence to the PSF record-filtration construction.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06588 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06588

### L-2026-002 — Maity, Onggadinata & Koh, “Exact Tradeoff Between Quantum Error Correction and Quantum Darwinism: An Information-Theoretic No-Go Theorem” (2026)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct/adjacent.
- **PSF claims touched:** N2, N5, P1, P5.
- **What overlaps:** Gives an exactly solvable system-environment model in which conditional environment-state overlap factorizes as cos^m(2 g_Z t), the full decoherence factor is Γ_N=cos^N(2 g_Z t), and Darwinistic redundancy is quantitatively tied to logical fidelity. It therefore occupies the same quantitative record/coherence territory as PSF, though with different observables and goals.
- **What differs:** Its tradeoff is between QEC logical fidelity, Holevo information, and Darwinistic redundancy; it does not introduce PSF's accessible-past filtration or selector law, and it remains ordinary unitary dynamics. The model is especially useful as a null/reference model for a PSF echo test rather than as a direct duplicate of PSF theorems.
- **Novelty threat:** medium to broad “redundancy versus coherence/recoverability” language; theorem-level threat to PSF's specific root-fidelity bounds remains unresolved pending direct comparison.
- **Useful contribution:** Supplies a fresh analytically solvable product-record model and a model-independent no-go bound; also gives a clean parameterization for a temporal echo discriminator.
- **Reopen if:** comparison shows one of PSF's quantitative bounds is a direct corollary/equivalent form of the new tradeoff.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03944 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03944

### L-2026-003 — Strasberg, Schindler, Wang & Winter, “Approximate Decoherence, Recoherence and Records in Isolated Quantum Systems” (2026)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct/adjacent.
- **PSF claims touched:** N5, P1, RT-2026-004.
- **What overlaps:** Treats records and decoherent histories while explicitly finding recoherence in isolated unitary systems; uses recovery ideas to characterize when histories retain coherent structure.
- **What differs:** No PSF filtration or optional selector law. Its importance here is adversarial: records/decoherence do not by themselves imply irreversible settledness.
- **Novelty threat:** low to the filtration mathematics; high relevance to any physical interpretation that would infer irreversibility from record formation alone.
- **Useful contribution:** Strengthens the standard-unitary null hypothesis for PSF echo proposals.
- **Reopen if:** full text reveals a quantitative record-fidelity/recoherence theorem directly equivalent to a PSF bound.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19703

### L-2026-004 — Ferté, Farci & Cao, “Decoherent histories with(out) objectivity in a (broken) apparatus” (2025/2026)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** adjacent/direct to records and histories.
- **PSF claims touched:** C3, N1, N5, P1.
- **What overlaps:** Shows approximate decoherent histories can emerge in both an apparatus/objective phase and an encoding/scrambling phase, so decoherent histories and objectivity are distinct notions. Fine-grained many-body observables can retain access to coherence even when coarse-grained histories appear classical.
- **What differs:** No increasing accessible-record filtration or PSF selector dynamics.
- **Novelty threat:** low to PSF's specific framework; strong red-team relevance to overinterpreting coarse-grained classicality as fundamental settlement.
- **Useful contribution:** Supports PSF's current insistence on separating record accessibility, history consistency, and stronger physical settledness.
- **Reopen if:** a direct theorem-level overlap with PSF's record conditions for consistent histories is identified.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16482 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2508.16482

### L-2026-005 — Riedel, Zurek & Zwolak, “The Objective Past of a Quantum Universe: Redundant Records of Consistent Histories” (2013)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct conceptual prior art.
- **PSF claims touched:** N1, P2.
- **What overlaps:** Explicitly characterizes an objective past through redundant, locally accessible records of histories and uses redundancy to select physically classical histories from merely consistent ones.
- **What differs:** Does not formulate the past as an increasing von Neumann-algebra filtration with compatible record states/expected lifts, nor the PSF visibility-envelope machinery.
- **Novelty threat:** high to any broad verbal claim that PSF first links “objective past” to redundant accessible records; medium to the narrower mathematical synthesis.
- **Useful contribution:** Forces novelty language to be structural and theorem-specific rather than philosophical/semantic.
- **Reopen if:** its partial-trace/redundant-consistency conditions can be mapped directly onto PSF's record realization or filtration axioms.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0331

### L-2026-006 — Riedel, Zurek & Zwolak, “The Rise and Fall of Redundancy in Decoherence and Quantum Darwinism” (2012)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct red-team prior art.
- **PSF claims touched:** N5, P1, RT-2026-004.
- **What overlaps:** Studies creation and subsequent loss of redundant branching records under unitary many-body dynamics.
- **What differs:** No PSF filtration or new dynamics.
- **Novelty threat:** low to PSF mathematics; strong counterevidence to treating redundancy itself as permanently irreversible.
- **Useful contribution:** Establishes that “record existed” and “record remains redundantly accessible forever” are distinct statements under standard dynamics.
- **Reopen if:** selector physics is reformulated in terms of redundancy persistence rather than stored pre-inverse record evidence.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.3197

### L-2026-007 — Zhu et al., “Observation of Quantum Darwinism and the Origin of Classicality with Superconducting Circuits” (2025)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** direct experimental-platform relevance.
- **PSF claims touched:** N5, P1.
- **What overlaps:** Realizes branching states and redundant environmental records with controlled conditional unitaries on a superconducting processor; the reported branching/decoherence is generated by unitary evolution. Uses up to ten environment qubits in the principal experiment.
- **What differs:** It tests Quantum Darwinism, not PSF; no selector degree of freedom or residual post-inverse visibility law is assumed.
- **Novelty threat:** none to PSF-specific new physics; provides a practical architecture for an echo/null experiment.
- **Useful contribution:** Demonstrates that tunable redundant-record formation is experimentally accessible in a controlled multi-qubit platform.
- **Reopen if:** an explicit inverse/Loschmidt echo was already performed in the same platform or follow-up work reports one.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00781 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2504.00781

### L-2026-008 — Schindler et al., “Undoing a quantum measurement” (2012)
- **Date assessed:** 2026-08-10
- **Relevance:** adjacent empirical null.
- **PSF claims touched:** N5, P1.
- **What overlaps:** Experimentally demonstrates deterministic reversal of a fully projective measurement on one particle when quantum information is protected/distributed through a three-particle QEC protocol.
- **What differs:** This is not a macroscopic redundant-record echo and does not test PSF.
- **Novelty threat:** none to PSF framework; strong methodological reminder that “measurement-like” information transfer can be reversible when the full relevant state remains controllable.
- **Useful contribution:** Supports using a genuine inverse as the null boundary rather than assuming measurement irreversibility.
- **Reopen if:** later experiments scale deterministic reversal to larger redundant record environments.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1791
